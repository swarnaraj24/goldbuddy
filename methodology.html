Data Sources & Calculation Methodology

1. Gold Rate Data Pipeline & Fallback Logic

GoldBuddy retrieves Indian 22K and 24K per-gram retail rates through an automated backend pipeline:

  • Primary Retail Sources: The backend queries public Indian retail gold rate boards (GoodReturns India retail board, with Groww India retail board as secondary retail source).
  • Spot-Parity Fallback: Only if both retail boards are temporarily unreachable or time out, GoldBuddy computes a baseline estimate from international spot gold (XAU/USD via gold-api.com) and the USD/INR exchange rate (via Frankfurter), converting troy ounces to grams (÷ 31.1034768) and applying a configurable Indian landed-parity multiplier (accounting for import duty and local bullion premiums).
  • GST & Update Cadence: Stored base rates exclude GST; toggling With 3% GST multiplies the base rate by 1.03. Live fetches are cached and rate-limited to 30-minute intervals to ensure stability.

4. 15-Day Scenario Projections (With Example)

The 15-day shopping date estimator projects three mathematical scenarios (Optimistic, Trend, and Risk) by clamping the trailing 5-day daily drift rate between -0.45% and +0.45% per day, scaling by √days, and bounding outputs within ±3.5% of the 5-day average.

Illustrative Numerical Example:

Suppose 22K gold is currently ₹12,000/g with a 5-day average of ₹12,100/g and a recent 5-day change of -1.0% (-0.20%/day). For a shopping date 9 days ahead (√9 = 3), the Trend Scenario scales by -0.20% × 3 = -0.60% to approximately ₹11,928/g, while Optimistic and Risk scenarios frame a wider short-term range around that baseline.

These scenarios are mathematical illustrations based on recent observations. They have not been established as statistically calibrated forecasts and should not be interpreted as probabilities.

3. Market Positioning & 5-Day Average

The Market Positioning indicator compares the current 22K per-gram rate against the trailing 5-day average recorded in our database. When the current price trades below the 5-day average, it is labeled as a relative dip signal; when above, it is labeled as above the recent average. This is a descriptive comparison of historical data—not a statistical probability of future returns.

4. 15-Day Scenario Projections

The 15-day shopping date estimator projects three mathematical scenarios (Optimistic, Trend, and Risk) by scaling the clamped 5-day momentum rate using square-root time scaling bounded within ±3.5% of the 5-day average. These ranges illustrate potential short-term variability and should never be treated as guaranteed future prices.
