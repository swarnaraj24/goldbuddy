How 3% GST Is Calculated on Gold in India

In India, physical gold, gold coins, digital gold, and gold jewellery attract a flat 3% Goods and Services Tax (GST) (1.5% CGST + 1.5% SGST on intrastate purchases).

Does 3% GST Apply to Making Charges Too?

Yes. When you purchase finished gold jewellery as a composite supply, 3% GST is calculated on the sum of the gold value plus making charges. For example, on ₹1,00,000 of gold and ₹10,000 of making charges (₹1,10,000 total), 3% GST is ₹3,300.

What About Exchanging Old Gold?

When an individual exchanges old personal gold jewellery for new jewellery at a registered showroom, GST is generally charged on the net differential value and new making charges—ask your jeweller for an itemized tax invoice showing the old-gold exchange credit clearly.
