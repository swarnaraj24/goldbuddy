How to Calculate the Price of Gold Jewellery in India

When you walk into a jewellery showroom in India, the final invoice is higher than weight × daily gold rate. Understanding the itemized billing structure helps you verify gross weight, net gold weight, making charges, wastage terminology, and 3% GST before paying.

The Standard Showroom Formula

Final Invoice = [ (Net Gold Weight × 22K Rate) + Making Charges + Stone/Bead Charges ] × 1.03 (3% GST)

Gross Weight vs. Net Weight & Wastage

Gross weight is the total scale weight of the ornament including stones, enamel, or thread. Net gold weight is the weight of the gold metal alone—gold per-gram rates should apply only to net gold weight. Some retailers also quote wastage (VA — Value Addition) alongside or bundled inside making charges; always request a written breakup of net gold weight and total VA/making fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should stones be weighed at the 22K gold rate?

No. Under standard consumer billing transparency, stone weight is deducted from gross weight so 22K gold rates apply only to net gold weight, with stones billed separately if applicable.
