Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information GoldBuddy collects, how it is used, and your choices when using our calculators and guides.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest Usage (Local Storage): You can use GoldBuddy's calculators and guides without creating an account. If you create a buying plan while signed out, your target levels are stored only in your own browser's localStorage.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in via email/password or Google OAuth to sync your 3-part buying plan and receive price-drop alerts, we store your account identifier, email address, saved target prices, and alert logs securely in Supabase.

2. How We Use Information

We use account data solely to authenticate your session, display your private buying plan, enforce fair daily rate-refresh limits, and send price-drop alert notifications you explicitly configured. We never sell or rent user email addresses or personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services & Affiliate Links

GoldBuddy relies on Supabase (authentication and database), Cloudflare Turnstile (bot protection), and third-party affiliate partners. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Clicking an outbound partner link takes you to that merchant's website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Data Deletion

You may delete individual buying-plan targets directly inside the GoldBuddy dashboard at any time, or clear your browser storage to remove guest data.
