How to Check BIS Hallmark & 6-Digit HUID on Gold Jewellery

Under Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmarking guidelines, hallmarked gold jewellery sold in covered districts in India carries three laser-inscribed marks along with a unique 6-character Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) number.

The 3 Mandatory Hallmark Symbols

  1. BIS Standard Triangle Mark: Confirms the piece was tested at a BIS-recognized Assaying & Hallmarking Centre.
  2. Purity & Fineness Grade: Such as 22K916 (91.6% pure gold), 18K750 (75% pure gold), or 14K585 (58.5% pure gold).
  3. 6-Character Alphanumeric HUID Code: A unique ID (letters and numbers) assigned to that individual ornament.

How to Verify HUID Before Paying

Ask the showroom staff to show you the HUID stamp with a magnifying loupe and print the HUID code on your invoice. Open the official government BIS Care mobile app, tap Verify HUID, and enter the 6-character code—it displays the registered jeweller's name, article category (ring, chain, bangle), and certified purity.
