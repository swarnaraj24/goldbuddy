How 3% GST Is Calculated on Gold in India

Under India's Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework, physical gold, gold coins, and gold jewellery attract a standard 3% GST (split into 1.5% CGST + 1.5% SGST for intrastate sales).

Does 3% GST Apply to Making Charges Too?

Yes. When you purchase finished gold jewellery as a composite supply, 3% GST is levied on the combined transaction value of the gold plus making charges. For example, on ₹1,00,000 of gold and ₹10,000 of making charges (₹1,10,000 subtotal), 3% GST equals ₹3,300.

Old-Gold Exchange & Digital Gold Notes

Taxation on old-gold exchanges and digital gold platforms can depend on how the transaction is structured (for example, whether an unregistered individual exchanges old personal gold for new jewellery versus a standalone sale, or how a digital gold provider structures storage and redemption fees). Always request an itemized GST tax invoice from your jeweller or platform and consult a qualified tax advisor for specific tax questions.
