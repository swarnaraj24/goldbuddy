About Us

About GoldBuddy

GoldBuddy is an independent Indian gold rate tracking and purchase-planning platform built to help everyday households make clearer, calmer gold-buying decisions.

Buying gold in India—whether for weddings, festivals, gifting, or long-term savings—often involves confusing showroom arithmetic: 22K vs. 24K purity conversions, percentage or per-gram making charges, wastage, and 3% GST applied on top of both gold and making fees.

Who Builds & Maintains GoldBuddy?

GoldBuddy is an independently developed digital utility maintained by the GoldBuddy Editorial & Product Team in India. We built GoldBuddy after experiencing firsthand how difficult it is to compare showroom quotes, understand making-charge markups, and plan multi-part gold purchases without hype or pressure.

Our Editorial & Tool Approach

We focus on transparent mathematical formulas, practical calculators, and educational consumer guides rather than promising future gold prices or claiming a guaranteed buying bottom. Every calculation states its underlying assumptions—such as making-charge percentage and 3% GST—and encourages buyers to verify final weights, BIS HUID hallmarks, and billing terms directly with their jeweller before completing any purchase.

Review Cadence & Corrections Policy

  • What GoldBuddy Offers: Live 22K & 24K rate benchmarks, instant showroom bill & budget calculators, a 3-part staggered buying planner, and plain-English consumer guides.
  • Regular Reviews: Calculator formulas, tax rules (such as the 3% GST structure), and educational articles are reviewed periodically to keep pace with Indian bullion and hallmarking standards.
  • Corrections Policy: If you spot a calculation edge case, outdated tax reference, or display issue, please report it through our Contact & Feedback page. Verified corrections are updated promptly.
