22K vs 24K Gold: Which Should You Buy in India?

Karat measures how many parts out of 24 are pure gold. Choosing between 22K and 24K depends on whether you are buying wearable ornaments or accumulating pure metal weight.

Feature 22K Gold (916 Hallmark) 24K Gold (999 Fine Gold)
Pure Gold Content91.67% gold + 8.33% alloy99.9% pure gold
DurabilityStrong; holds clasps & shapes wellSoft; bends or scratches easily
Best Used ForRings, chains, bangles, wedding setsGold coins, bullion bars, digital gold
Typical Making Charges8% – 25%2% – 5% (minting fee on coins)
GST Rate3% on gold + making3% on coin/bar value

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 22K cheaper per gram than 24K?

Because 22K contains 22/24ths (91.67%) pure gold, its base rate is roughly 8.33% lower per gram than 24K before making charges are added.

Can I exchange 22K jewellery for 24K coins later?

Most major Indian jewellers allow upgrading or exchanging hallmarked 22K gold at prevailing daily bullion rates, deducting only the original making charges and GST.
