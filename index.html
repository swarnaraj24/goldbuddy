Why Is Gold Moving Like This?

Cooling Trend • Accumulation Window

Gold is currently pulling back from recent highs. When international bullion dips while the rupee holds steady, staggered buyers get an opportunity to accumulate in smaller slices before local retail counters rebound.

Sticky US inflation keeps rate-hike expectations alive. Higher bond yields pull short-term money away from non-yielding gold.

A firmer US Dollar Index (DXY) makes dollar-denominated XAU/USD costlier globally, pressuring international spot quotes lower.
