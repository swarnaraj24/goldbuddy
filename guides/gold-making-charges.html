How Gold Jewellery Making Charges Work in India

Making charges represent the labour, design complexity, and finishing required to craft gold into wearable jewellery. Because making charges are generally not recovered when selling or exchanging jewellery later, comparing quotes across retailers can materially change your total cost.

Jewellery Category Illustrative Range* Pricing Notes
24K Gold Coins / Bars~2% – 5%Minting & tamper-proof assay packaging
Machine-Made Plain Chains~6% – 12%Often quoted per gram or lower %
Handcrafted Bangles & Rings~12% – 18%Varies with filigree & polish work
Antique / Temple / Stone~18% – 28%+Confirm net gold vs. stone/lac weight

*Note: Ranges shown above are illustrative market examples as of 2026 and are not fixed industry tariffs. Actual making charges vary by retailer, city, brand, and craftsmanship.

Percentage vs. Flat Per-Gram Making Charges

When gold prices rise sharply, a percentage-based making charge (e.g., 14%) automatically increases in rupee terms even though the artisan's labour remains the same. Ask whether your jeweller offers a flat ₹/gram rate on plain designs, and test different making-charge percentages on the GoldBuddy Calculator.
