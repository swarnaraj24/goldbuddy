Quick Reference Notes

Gold Coins vs. Gold Jewellery

24K 999.9 coins and bars typically carry 2%–5% making charges, making them efficient for pure weight accumulation, whereas 22K jewellery carries 8%–25% craftsmanship charges for wearability.

How Much Gold Can ₹50,000 Buy?

At ₹12,000/g (22K) with 10% making and 3% GST, effective cost is ₹13,596/g—so ₹50,000 buys approximately 3.677 grams. Check live numbers on the Budget Calculator.

What Moves Daily Gold Prices in India?

Daily retail gold rates respond to international spot gold (XAU/USD), the USD/INR exchange rate, central bank demand, and domestic seasonal wedding demand.

Staggered Gold Buying Strategy

Splitting a planned budget into 3 parts (30% / 40% / 30%) across graduated price targets reduces the risk of buying 100% of your gold at a short-term peak.
