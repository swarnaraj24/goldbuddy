22K vs 24K Gold: Which Should You Buy in India?

Karat measures how many parts out of 24 are pure gold. Choosing between 22K and 24K depends on whether you are buying wearable ornaments or accumulating pure metal weight.

Feature 22K Gold (916 Hallmark) 24K Gold (999 Fine Gold)
Pure Gold Content91.67% gold + 8.33% alloy99.9% pure gold
DurabilityStronger; holds clasps & shapes wellSoft; bends or scratches easily
Best Used ForRings, chains, bangles, wedding setsGold coins, bullion bars, digital gold
Illustrative Making Range~8% – 25% (varies by design & retailer)~2% – 5% (minting/packaging fee)
GST Rate3% on gold + making3% on coin/bar value

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 22K cheaper per gram than 24K?

Because 22K contains 22/24ths (91.67%) pure gold, its base metal rate is approximately 8.33% lower per gram than 24K before making charges are added.

How do resale and exchange policies work?

Buyback and exchange terms vary significantly by jeweller, purity documentation, and whether you exchange for new jewellery or cash. Typically, original making charges and GST are not refunded, and non-hallmarked or third-party pieces may incur melting or purity-testing deductions—always check the jeweller's written buyback policy before buying.
