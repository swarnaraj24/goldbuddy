What Is 1 Pavan (Sovereign) of Gold in Grams?

In Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and across South India, gold jewellery weight is traditionally measured in Pavan (also spelled Pavun, Savaran, or Sovereign). 1 Pavan of gold equals exactly 8 grams.

Equals: 8.00 grams
