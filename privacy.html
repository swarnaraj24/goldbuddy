Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what categories of information GoldBuddy collects, how each category is used and retained, and how you can access or delete your data.

1. Categories of Information We Collect

  • Guest Browser Storage (localStorage): You can use GoldBuddy's calculators and guides without signing in. If you create a buying plan while signed out, your target prices and daily refresh counters are stored locally in your own browser and are not transmitted as personal profile records.
  • Account & Google OAuth Data (Optional): If you choose to create an account via email/password or sign in with Google OAuth, we receive your authentication user ID and email address (and basic profile name if provided by Google) solely to authenticate your account and sync your saved data.
  • Buying Plans & Price-Drop Alerts: When signed in, your saved target rates, budget allocations, and triggered alert history are stored privately under your user ID in our database.
  • Contact & Feedback Submissions: When you submit a message on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, selected topic, and message content so we can review and respond to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly to operate GoldBuddy: authenticating your account, syncing your 3-part buying plan, sending requested price-drop email alerts, enforcing fair rate-refresh limits, and responding to support messages. We never sell, rent, or trade user personal information to data brokers.

4. Data Retention

  • Account & Buying Plan Records: Saved buying-plan targets and alert logs are retained while your account remains active so your preferences stay synced across sessions, and are removed upon verified account deletion.
  • Contact Form Messages: Inquiries submitted through our Contact page are retained for up to 12 months (or as needed to resolve your request) before periodic cleanup.
  • Security & Rate-Limit Logs: Basic technical timestamps (such as daily fetch counts) may be retained temporarily to prevent abuse and protect service availability.

5. Account and Personal Data Deletion

You can delete individual buying-plan targets directly inside the dashboard or clear guest data via your browser storage settings at any time.

If you have created a GoldBuddy account and would like to request deletion of your account and associated personal data (or request access or correction), please contact us through our Contact page using the Account & Data Deletion Request topic from your registered email address. We will review and process requests in accordance with applicable requirements and any necessary data-retention obligations. Account-related information, saved buying plans, and alert records will be handled through our account deletion process.
