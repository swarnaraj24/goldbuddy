About GoldBuddy

GoldBuddy is an independent Indian gold rate tracking and purchase-planning platform built to help everyday households make clearer, calmer gold-buying decisions.

Buying gold in India—whether for weddings, festivals, gifting, or long-term savings—often involves confusing showroom arithmetic: 22K vs. 24K purity conversions, percentage or per-gram making charges, wastage, and 3% GST applied on top of both gold and making fees.

What GoldBuddy Offers

  • Live 22K & 24K Rate Board: View per-gram, 8g (1 Pavan), and 10g Indian retail benchmarks with or without 3% GST.
  • Showroom Bill & Budget Calculator: Instantly translate a fixed rupee budget into buyable grams, or calculate a full jewellery invoice including making charges and GST.
  • Staggered 3-Part Buying Planner: Instead of guessing a single market bottom, split a planned purchase into three price levels (30% / 40% / 30%) and track target dips.
  • Educational Gold Knowledge Hub: Plain-English guides on BIS HUID hallmark verification, purity standards, making charge structures, and gold taxation.
