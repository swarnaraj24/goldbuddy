Bill Calculation Guide

How to Calculate the Price of Gold Jewellery in India

When you walk into a jewellery showroom in India, the final invoice is always higher than weight × daily gold rate. Understanding the exact billing formula protects you from overpaying on making charges or stone weights.

The Standard Showroom Formula

Final Invoice = [ (Net Gold Weight × 22K Rate) + Making Charges + Stone Charges ] × 1.03 (3% GST)

Worked Example: 10g 22K Necklace

Bill Component Calculation Amount (₹)
Net 22K Gold Value10g × ₹12,000/g₹1,20,000
Making Charges (12%)12% of ₹1,20,000₹14,400
SubtotalGold + Making₹1,34,400
3% GST3% of ₹1,34,400₹4,032
Final Payable BillAll-inclusive₹1,38,432

Frequently Asked Questions

Should stones be weighed with gold?

No. Always ask for the net gold weight separate from gross weight so you are not paying 22K gold rates for semi-precious stones or beads.

Can I test my budget before visiting a store?
