What Is 1 Pavan (Sovereign) of Gold in Grams?

In Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and across South India, gold jewellery weight is traditionally measured in Pavan (also spelled Pavun, Savaran, or Sovereign). 1 Pavan of gold equals exactly 8 grams.

Pavan / Sovereign Weight in Grams Common Jewellery Example
1/2 Pavan (Ara Pavan)4 gramsLight ring or ear studs
1 Pavan (1 Sovereign)8 gramsDaily-wear chain or single bangle
2 Pavan16 gramsPair of medium gold bangles
5 Pavan40 gramsMid-weight necklace set
10 Pavan80 gramsBridal haram or multi-piece wedding set
