How Gold Jewellery Making Charges Work in India

Making charges represent the labour, design complexity, and finishing required to turn raw gold into wearable jewellery. Because making charges are non-recoverable upon resale, comparing them across jewellers is one of the easiest ways to save money.

Jewellery Category Typical Making Range Pricing Notes
24K Gold Coins / Bars2% – 5%Lowest overhead; machine-stamped
Machine-Made Plain Chains6% – 12%Often quoted per gram or low %
Handcrafted Bangles & Rings12% – 18%Varies with filigree & polish work
Antique / Temple / Kundan18% – 28%+Check net gold vs. stone/lac weight

Percentage vs. Flat Per-Gram Making Charges

When gold prices rise sharply, a percentage-based making charge (e.g., 14%) automatically becomes more expensive in rupee terms even though the artisan's labour hasn't changed. Ask whether your jeweller offers a flat ₹/gram rate on plain designs.
